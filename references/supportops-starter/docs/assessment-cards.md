# Assessment cards

These are tutor contracts. During Assess, reveal the task but not the acceptance answer.

## J1 — Structured intake

Use an unfamiliar support message containing at least one absent fact and one language feature such as negation, uncertainty, chronology, or instruction-like text. Ask the learner to predict/explain the structured result, modify one field/rule, and identify which check catches a schema-valid unsupported claim.

Pass evidence distinguishes unknown from false, preserves attribution, and explains why schema validity is not source truth.

## J2 — Transfer

Give a delivery dispute with customer and supplied carrier-status statements that conflict. Ask the learner to adapt intake without adding a live integration. Pass evidence preserves both sources, does not silently declare either universal truth, and modifies the prior mechanism rather than building a second unrelated app.

## J3 — Retrieval

Provide a query where an older/superseded policy contains tempting matching terms. Ask the learner to diagnose eligible-document filtering and retrieval before generation. Pass evidence locates the failing layer and preserves provenance.

## J4 — Tools/approval

Use an unfamiliar wrong-scope lookup or changed proposal after approval. Pass evidence keeps trusted identity outside model control and requires a new approval when target/parameters/version changed. A rejected/missing/stale/replayed approval produces no simulated effect.

## J5 — Ownership

Without worked-answer help, ask the learner to explain one representative data path, make one unfamiliar change, debug one unfamiliar failure, and transfer one key rule to a new case. Execution may have prior support; final ownership claims require the assistance levels defined in `docs/progress-protocol.md`.
